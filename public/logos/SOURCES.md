# University emblem sources

The numbered filenames match game levels 1–11.

1. University of Chinese Academy of Sciences — `国科大校徽.svg`, Chinese Wikipedia file repository; the circular emblem was cropped from the official horizontal mark for in-ball legibility.
2. Tsinghua University — `Tsinghua University Logo.svg`, Wikimedia Commons, public domain.
3. Peking University — `Peking University seal.svg`, Wikimedia Commons, public domain.
4. Fudan University — `Fudan University Logo.svg`, Wikimedia Commons, public domain.
5. Shanghai Jiao Tong University — `SJTU emblem.svg`, Chinese Wikipedia file repository.
6. Zhejiang University — `Zhejiang University Logo.svg`, Chinese Wikipedia file repository.
7. Beijing Institute of Technology — `Beijing Institute of Technology Logo.svg`, Chinese Wikipedia file repository.
8. Renmin University of China — `Renmin University of China logo.svg`, Chinese Wikipedia file repository.
9. Harbin Institute of Technology — `Harbin Institute of Technology logo.svg`, Chinese Wikipedia file repository.
10. Wuhan University — `WHU Logo 2022Ver.svg`, Chinese Wikipedia file repository; identity cross-checked against [Wuhan University's official visual identity page](https://www.whu.edu.cn/xxgk/wdbs.htm).
11. Xi'an Jiaotong University — official emblem image from [Xi'an Jiaotong University's school identity page](https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/info/1024/5221.htm).

University emblems may also be protected as trademarks; they are used here only to identify the corresponding universities in the game.
